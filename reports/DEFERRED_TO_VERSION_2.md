# Deferred to SANDARBH Version 2

Version 1.1.0 — 2026-10-07

No Version 2 experiment was performed during V1.1. The following require a new protocol, fresh development process, and—where applicable—a new locked test:

- A 2×2 hard/soft × unweighted/weighted loss ablation to isolate soft labels from cost weighting.
- Training-aware uncertainty that incorporates seeds and optimization variability, not only fixed-model cluster bootstrap uncertainty.
- New calibration methods and thresholds; V1 temperatures and 0.60 remain frozen.
- Broader paraphrase, thread, author, and semantic leakage controls.
- Independent-domain, temporal, demographic, dialect, reclaimed-language, quotation, negation, sarcasm, and community-led evaluation.
- Community-validated error taxonomy, fairness protocol, and harm assessment.
- Explicit missing-context modeling and controlled context-reasoning probes.
- Dataset-author resolution of the 122 empty rows and 242-versus-256 discrepancy, plus byte-level provenance.
- Any deployment, moderation, or commercial study, subject to author permission and separate governance.
- Explanations or rewrite generation; neither belongs to V1.1.
